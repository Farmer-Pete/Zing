Prototype L: Hybrid Zones

Three-zone approach: info strips + quick-action bar + overflow menu

Concept: This is a pragmatic hybrid of the best ideas from the other prototypes. The card has three distinct zones:

Zone 1 — Status strips: Purely informational. PR links, CI dots, session indicators, findings count. Zero buttons. Stays clean and scannable.

Zone 2 — Quick-action bar: A small footer bar with at most 2 buttons: the computed primary action (from Prototype G's state machine) and optionally one secondary. Always visible — no hover required.

Zone 3 — Overflow menu: A ⋮ button in the quick-action bar that opens a dropdown with all remaining actions (from Prototype F). Grouped by category.

Rules: Zone 2 never has more than 2 action buttons + the overflow. The primary button's label and color change based on card state. If a card has no meaningful actions (e.g., completed/merged), Zone 2 is omitted entirely.
