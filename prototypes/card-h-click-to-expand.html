Prototype H: Click to Expand

Zero buttons when collapsed. Click card to reveal full detail panel.

Concept: The collapsed card is purely informational — it shows a compact summary line with chips (PR status, session state, CI dots) but zero action buttons. Clicking anywhere on the card expands an inline detail panel with full PR info, session details, and all actions organized by section.

Key benefit: The kanban board stays visually clean. Cards are compact and scan-friendly. Actions only appear when you've committed attention to a specific card.

Trade-off: Every action requires two interactions: click to expand, then click the action. Power users might find this slower. The expanded card takes more vertical space.
