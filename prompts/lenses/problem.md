## In a plan
Ask, in this order:
- Is this worth doing, and why now? The problem element must say what is
  broken or missing and who is affected.
- Do the goals follow from the problem, and does the design reach every goal?
- Is each non-goal a real exclusion the owner would recognise?
- Is the working demo the right 20 percent: the smallest slice that shows
  the value end to end?
- Is each alternative real, and is the reason it lost stated?
- Does anything in the plan exceed the ticket?
- For a bug: is the proof satisfactory? Each claim about the cause and
  the fix has a recorded command and output, and that output shows what
  the claim says it shows.
